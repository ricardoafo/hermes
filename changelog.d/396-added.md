- **Total IRPF view by region** (`show_total_irpf: true` in the taxpayer profile, off by default): `hermes tax` adds the year's general-base tax (state plus regional scale, personal minimum relieved at the bottom rates per Ley 35/2006 arts. 63 and 74), the full savings tax, the hermes share and the total, labelled an estimate before quota deductions. `config/tax_scales.yaml` gains `general.state` and `general.regions.murcia` tables for 2025 and 2026, each cited to the BOE text; the profile gains `region` and per-year `general_deductions_eur`, which also lowers the general base used for the personal-minimum remainder. A missing region or scale prints "total IRPF unavailable: <reason>"; the hermes figure is identical with the view on or off. Closes #396.
