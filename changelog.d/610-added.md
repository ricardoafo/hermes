- **Console on the VPS from your PC** (`hermes --vps <command>`): runs a read-only console command (`tui`, `status`, `positions`, `activity`, `logs`) on the VPS over `ssh -t` and shows it locally, with one-line errors for no ssh, unreachable host, auth failure and a VPS that needs a deploy. Host, user and key come from `HERMES_VPS_HOST`, `HERMES_VPS_USER`, `HERMES_VPS_KEY`. Closes #610.
