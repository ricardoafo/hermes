- **`hermes tax-pack`** (`src/hermes/application/filing/pack.py`, `src/hermes/entrypoints/tax_pack.py`): keeps one tax filing pack per year up to date as hermes trades. Each pack holds the Modelo 100 and 721 figures, a trail from every sale to its lots, fees and ECB rates, a readiness checklist, a dated log of changed figures and a checksum manifest. A closed year whose pack is ready freezes. The estimate is added only where the taxpayer profile exists. A daily `hermes-tax-pack.timer` (07:30 Madrid) ships switched off. Nothing is submitted to the AEAT (#568).
